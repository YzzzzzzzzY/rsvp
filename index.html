Chapter I

Friends, then something more

We knew each other long before we fell in love.

We crossed paths in high school, then truly became friends in 2021. What grew slowly was never rushed — just conversations, shared meals, and the feeling that the ordinary days were better together.

Chapter II

A rooftop, a cold night

The night everything became clear.

On November 11, 2021, Yan found Zhen on a rooftop — flowers, careful words, and the courage to say yes to something real. It was not dramatic for the sake of it. It was deliberate, warm, and ours.

Chapter III

Music, miles, and inside jokes

The small things that became our language.

Between Michigan winters, California summers, and flights booked on a whim, we collected songs, road trips, and ridiculous ideas. The wedding will have a little of that — not a performance, just us.

Chapter IV

A quiet yes

When the question finally came.

There was no flash mob — just a moment that felt inevitable. A song, a ring hidden in plain sight, and the relief of knowing we had chosen each other on purpose.

Chapter V

A garden in Menlo Park

Where we want to say our vows.

Allied Arts Guild felt right immediately — old stone, green lawns, art studios tucked into the trees. We wanted somewhere beautiful but unhurried, where friends could wander and linger.

Chapter VI

Old friends, full hearts

What we are most excited about.

More than anything, we are excited to see people we love in one place — the friends who watched this story unfold, the family who raised us, and the faces we have missed.
